# USB-UIRT

A long-established, fully assembled USB IR receiver/transmitter from usbuirt.com. Roughly **$55** plus **$10–30 shipping**, built to order.

## Capabilities

- Send and receive IR from a PC.
- Linux support through LIRC's `usb_uirt_raw` driver.
- Mature product with a long history in home-theatre automation.

## Pros

- Assembled, no soldering.
- Both TX and RX.
- Established product name.

## Cons

- Expensive relative to the task, especially with shipping.
- Build-to-order lead time.
- The vendor has posted notices that **Windows driver support is being deprecated in 2026** due to SHA-1 signing. Linux is unaffected, but the notice adds uncertainty and noise.
- Direct-order only, not a normal high-street retailer.

## Verdict

Workable but poor value for one IR command, and not a mainstream retail purchase. Rejected in favour of [[Adafruit-GPIO-IR-SELECTED]].
